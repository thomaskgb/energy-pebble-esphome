Energy Pebble setup

Plug in your Energy Pebble. When the middle light slowly pulses blue, it is ready to be connected to your Wi-Fi.

Connect with one tap

Your browser can talk to the Pebble directly. Tap the button, pick energy-pebble from the list, then choose your Wi-Fi network and enter its password.

Your browser is now scanning: a window lists nearby Pebbles. Make sure yours is plugged in, close by, and slowly pulsing blue, then select energy-pebble and tap Pair. If the list stays empty or you closed the window, unplug the Pebble, plug it back in, and tap the button again — or use the manual steps below.

Doesn't work? Use the manual steps below — they work on every phone.

Connect via the setup network

  1. On your phone, open Settings → Wi-Fi.
  2. Join the network called Energy Pebble (no password).
  3. A setup page opens by itself. If it doesn't, open your browser and go to 192.168.4.1.
  4. Pick your home Wi-Fi network, type its password, and press Save.
  5. Your phone will drop off the setup network — that's normal. The Pebble is now joining your Wi-Fi.

What the middle light means

Blue, slow pulse Waiting for setup — follow the steps above
Yellow, slow pulse Connecting to your Wi-Fi
Red, slow blink Couldn't connect — password may be wrong. Redo the steps above
Steady colors Done! The Pebble is showing energy prices

Once connected, prices update automatically. Dashboard: energypebble.tdlx.nl
